At golden sunset a towering wall of horses made of pouring sand erupts out of a
small playground sandbox and surges into the sky, sand streaming from their
manes and hooves. At its base a small laughing girl holds up a hand-lettered
cardboard sign that reads MADE YOU LOOK in big clean black marker letters, the
swings and slide dwarfed behind her.

## Run it

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
