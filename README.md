A warm 1970s kitchen at breakfast: the refrigerator door is blasted off its
hinges and a colossal polar bear lunges out of the fridge toward the table,
roaring. Frost and ice shards explode across the room, cereal and juice fly,
a family of three rocks back in their chairs, and cold blue light spills from
the fridge against the warm morning sun.

## Run it

```
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
