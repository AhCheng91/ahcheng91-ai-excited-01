The sky had been a sheet of glass, and then it was not. A colossal humpback whale
bursts through it and hangs mid-lunge above a desert highway at dusk, caught in
hard warm sunset rim light as glass shards rain down and glitter against a deep
indigo sky. Far below, a single tiny truck drives on, hilariously small against
the enormous creature suspended over the road.
